What could happen in one year

The curve shows where your money can be after one year. The red tail is the worst years (5% of them at 95%). Where the tail starts is the Value-at-Risk. It must stay right of your loss limit (dashed line).

How the answer is found

VaR(f) grows with the stock fraction f, without jumps. At f = 0 it is below your limit, at the top it is above, so by the Intermediate Value Theorem it crosses your limit somewhere. Bisection finds that point by halving the interval again and again:
